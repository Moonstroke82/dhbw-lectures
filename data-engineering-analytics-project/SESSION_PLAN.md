# Project Data Engineering and Analytics — Project Plan (DRAFT 2026-10-07, awaiting approval)

Semester 6, DSKI. 50 h contact time + 100 h self-study. Assessment: project.

**Set-up:** two partner companies, two projects; the course splits into two teams. The projects are introduced in session 1 of *Data Analytics* (semester 5), so teams have a head start. Semester 6 is implementation only: the plenary meetings are **kickoff, mid-term presentation and final presentation**. Company names appear only in the offline PPTX.

**Team size:** each team is about half the course, so too large for one undivided team. Proposal: each team organises itself into sub-teams (e.g. data engineering, analytics, product and project management) with a product owner contact at the company and rotating roles.

Legend for learning-outcome links: **SC** subject competence · **MC** methodological competence · **PSC** personal/social · **OC** overarching (see [MODULE.md](MODULE.md)).

## Part A — Preparation (semester 5, alongside Data Analytics)

| # | Milestone | Contents | Result | LO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prep | **Project pitches and team formation** | Partner companies present their projects in Data Analytics session 1; students choose a project; two teams | Team lists | PSC |
| Prep | **Project understanding** | Business goals and success criteria; stakeholder analysis; as-is process analysis; first data inventory; data access and confidentiality agreements; initial backlog | Short project brief | MC, PSC, OC |

## Part B — Implementation (semester 6)

| # | Milestone | Contents | Result | LO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Kickoff (3 h)** | Project charter, scope and goals; roles and sub-teams; project management approach (e.g. Scrum with sprints); working agreements and team building; toolchain (Git, code reviews, tests); assessment criteria | Project charter, sprint plan | MC, PSC |
| Sprint | **Data engineering sprints** | Requirements and architecture; ingestion pipelines; data model and storage; data quality checks; sprint reviews with the company | Working data pipeline | SC, MC |
| 2 | **Mid-term presentation (3 h)** | Each team presents status, architecture, first results, risks and plan; feedback from company and lecturer; course correction | Mid-term presentation | SC, PSC, OC |
| Sprint | **Analytics and integration sprints** | Analysis and models; evaluation; dashboard or data product; testing and integration; documentation and hand-over | Data product, documentation | SC, MC |
| 3 | **Final presentation (4 h)** | Results and demo for the partner companies; each student explains and defends their contribution; retrospective | Final presentation, code, documentation, individual reflection | SC, PSC, OC |

## Assessment (proposal, to be decided)

| Component | Form | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Project result | Data product, code repository, technical documentation | 40 % |
| Project management | Charter, backlog, sprint artefacts, risk and stakeholder management | 15 % |
| Presentations | Mid-term (10 %) and final presentation incl. demo (15 %) | 25 % |
| Individual contribution | Individual reflection on own role and team cooperation, own contribution defended in the final presentation; peer assessment as input | 20 % |

Individual grades can differ within a team through the individual component (handbook: students justify and defend their own contribution).

## Notes

- The plenary meetings add up to about 10 h. How the remaining contact time is counted (e.g. scheduled team work with the lecturer available for coaching, sprint reviews with the companies) is still to be decided.
