Project Data Engineering and Analytics

Two real projects with partner companies: teams build a data pipeline and an analytics solution from kickoff to final presentation.

Semester 6Two partner projectsKickoff · Mid-term · FinalProject

What you will learn

  • Carry out a data engineering and analytics project for a real partner
  • Select and apply suitable methods and combine them with project management
  • Work in a team towards a goal and reflect on your own role and the roles of others
  • Justify and defend your own contribution to the project
  • Understand and take into account the interests of different stakeholders
