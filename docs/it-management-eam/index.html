DHBW · Digital Business Management (Business IT)

IT Management and Enterprise Architecture Management

How companies steer IT strategically and operationally, and how enterprise architecture links business and IT.

Semester 455 hours19 sessionsWritten exam

What you will learn

  • Distinguish the fields of strategic and operational IT management
  • Apply methods for IT service management (ITIL), IT controlling and governance
  • Understand and discuss the main domains of an enterprise architecture
  • Plan and assess IT landscapes, integration and sourcing options
  • Work with EAM frameworks (TOGAF, ArchiMate) and tools (e.g. LeanIX)
  • Consider compliance and ethical requirements in IT decisions
